Installing on Android

  1. Open a game in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.
  3. Launch it from the home screen. It opens fullscreen and works offline.

Locking the toddler in

A web app can't block the Home or Recents gestures. Android's App pinning can: Settings → Security → App pinning (sometimes under "Other security settings"), turn it on, then open the game, go to Recents, tap the app's icon and choose Pin. To unpin, hold Back and Recents (or swipe up and hold).
